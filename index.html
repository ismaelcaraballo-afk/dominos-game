How to Play

  1. Each player draws 7 tiles from the shuffled set.
  2. The player with the highest double starts; otherwise the highest tile leads.
  3. On your turn, place a tile that matches an open end of the chain, or draw from the boneyard.
  4. If no play is possible and the boneyard is empty, pass.
  5. A round ends when a player plays all tiles, or when the game is blocked.
  6. The round winner scores the total pip count of all opponents' remaining tiles.
  7. First player to reach the points goal wins!
